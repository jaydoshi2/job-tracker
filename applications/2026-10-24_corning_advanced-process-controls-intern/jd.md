# Corning — Advanced Process Controls Intern (Req 78170)
Division: Manufacturing, Technology and Engineering (MT&E)

## Role Purpose
Apply controls, optimization, data analytics, and machine learning to real manufacturing challenges: model-based control, numerical optimization, process monitoring, fault detection, defect classification, and predictive maintenance.

## Key Responsibilities
- Support design, development, testing, and documentation of process controls solutions in multidisciplinary teams.
- Analyze process data, control performance, and manufacturing trends to improve stability, quality, throughput, or efficiency.
- Assist with controls-oriented modeling, system identification, simulation, and evaluation of PID and model-based control strategies.
- Contribute to model predictive control, internal model control, or other advanced-control concepts.
- Build prototype optimization or analytics models using MATLAB, Simulink, Python, or related packages.
- Apply data analytics, statistical methods, or ML to process monitoring, fault detection, defect classification, predictive maintenance, or continuous improvement.
- Work with process SMEs to translate requirements into technical tasks; communicate findings clearly.
- Prepare technical summaries, presentations, code, models, and documentation; present final project outcomes.

## Required
- Pursuing B.S./M.S./Ph.D. in EE, ME, ChemE, Systems Eng., Operations Research, CS, Data Science, Applied Math, or related.
- Foundational understanding of process controls, dynamic systems, statistics, optimization, or machine learning.
- Programming in Python, MATLAB/Simulink, or comparable.
- Analyze data, build models, test hypotheses, communicate findings; team and independent work; comfort in manufacturing environments.

## Desired
- Model predictive control, PID control, system identification, or simulation.
- Numerical optimization (LP, NLP, MILP, convex, solver-based tools).
- ML, statistical modeling, multivariate analysis, PCA, PLS regression, time-series analysis.
- Git, version control, data visualization, software development practices.
- Interest in manufacturing, applied research, advanced controls, operations research, industrial data science.
