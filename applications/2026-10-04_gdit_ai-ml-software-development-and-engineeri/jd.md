## Job description

GDIT's 10-week Summer Internship Program: work side-by-side with GDIT professionals, collaborate with peers on a business challenge, mentoring circle, leader interaction, professional development.

### How You'll Make this Internship Opportunity Your Own
Our AI/ML Software Development and Engineering summer interns will work on a range of projects that ideally align with their interests. Some interns will develop agentic solutions capable of answering business questions and performing approved actions across enterprise information sources. Other interns will develop standalone proxy services for full, front-end management of any LLM implementation. The ultimate product will be a single managed service which includes features such as detection, monitoring, security, and FinOps, allowing flexible implementations that nonetheless adhere to consistent governance and management standards. The ideal intern will have knowledge of AI/ML tools and fundamentals, software development and engineering best practices, API integration, microservice architecture, and at least one programming language.

### The Must-Haves
- Currently enrolled in an associate's, bachelor's or master's degree program in computer science, IT, engineering, or other STEM field from a United States-based college or university.
- Basic understanding of core Data Science concepts, including data cleaning, exploratory analysis, simple statistical methods, and foundational machine learning principles.
- Basic understanding of Microsoft Office products (Word, PowerPoint, SharePoint, Excel, Teams).

### What You'll Need to Deliver Your Best Every Day
- Problem solving and interest in big data; interest or experience with big data platform tools
- Data cleaning, wrangling, transformation, model selection, cross-validation, and deployment
- Building and maintaining scalable APIs and microservices
- AI/ML model performance measurement, validation testing, and error or bias troubleshooting
- Containerization, optimization, and workflow automation
- AI/ML security best practices: data provenance, isolation, monitoring, adversarial testing
- Responsible AI/ML governance: transparency, risk mitigation, fairness, compliance
- FinOps practices
- Knowledge of machine learning fundamentals
- Knowledge of Python, SQL, or other programming languages
- Knowledge of libraries like PyTorch, TensorFlow, or scikit-learn
- Experience working in a collaborative team environment
