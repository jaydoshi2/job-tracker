## About the role
Supports process optimization, yield improvement, throughput enhancement, cost reduction and issue resolution within a manufacturing area (DRAM fab expansion in Boise). Partners with equipment engineering, operations, process owners and cross-functional stakeholders.

## Responsibilities
- Drive process optimization and manufacturing efficiency: monitor process performance, analyze data, support throughput and wafer output goals.
- Identify, troubleshoot and resolve process issues (yield loss, defects, equipment restrictions, excursions); escalate when needed.
- Apply SPC, FDC, DOE and structured problem solving (8D) to improve process capability and equipment performance.
- Partner with process owners, operations and engineering on chronic issues and continuous improvement.
- Use AI and data analytics tools to find improvement opportunities and optimize wafer processing.

## Minimum qualifications
- Pursuing a BS/MS in ChemE, EE, Materials Science, Physics or related engineering discipline.
- Actively enrolled; not graduating before September 2026.
- Understanding of Statistical Process Control (SPC) and Design of Experiments (DOE).
- Proficiency in Python, Java, MATLAB, Tableau or Microsoft Office.
- Ability to analyze manufacturing/engineering data and make data-driven decisions.

## Preferred qualifications
- Communication and cross-team collaboration.
- Data collection systems, data warehousing, data cleansing.
- Data analytics: database querying, interpreting large datasets.
- Semiconductor fabrication processes, manufacturing equipment or fab systems.
- Organization/prioritization; applying AI or advanced analytics to operational/engineering outcomes.
