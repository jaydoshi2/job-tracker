## Job description

At apexanalytix, we're lifelong innovators! Since the date of our founding nearly four decades ago we've been consistently growing, profitable, and delivering the best procure-to-pay solutions to the world. We're the perfect balance of established company and start-up.

Are you passionate about cloud technology and ready to get your hands dirty with real enterprise infrastructure? APEX Analytix is looking for an enthusiastic Cloud Infrastructure Intern to join our team in Greensboro, NC.

### What You'll Be Doing

**Virtualization & Kubernetes**
- Get hands-on with KVM/Kubevirt virtual machine deployments alongside senior engineers.
- Dive into Kubernetes cluster configuration and health monitoring in real on-premises environments.
- Test and document Helm chart deployments and ingress configurations.
- Learn container security best practices and resource optimization in a live setting.

**Linux Administration**
- Take part in real Linux server administration: patching, log analysis, and performance monitoring.
- Apply OS hardening techniques using proven runbooks and security standards.
- Sharpen your troubleshooting instincts on Linux systems with guidance from experienced engineers.

**Automation & Monitoring**
- Build and maintain live monitoring dashboards in Prometheus and Grafana.
- Write Bash and Python automation scripts that actually get used.
- Contribute to real incident response workflows by gathering diagnostics and analyzing logs.

**Infrastructure & Hardware**
- Shadow senior engineers through hands-on server maintenance, firmware upgrades, and hardware diagnostics.
- Help manage inventory for servers, SAN/NAS storage, and network equipment in our data center.

**Documentation & Teamwork**
- Own and improve infrastructure documentation, runbooks, and internal wikis.
- Collaborate daily with DevOps and Engineering teams on real projects.

### What We're Looking For
- Currently pursuing or recently completed a degree in Computer Science, Information Technology, or a related field.
- Working knowledge of Linux (Ubuntu, CentOS, or RHEL).
- Familiarity with virtualization or containerization concepts such as VMs, Docker, or Kubernetes.
- Some scripting experience in Bash or Python.
- Strong communicator who can document clearly and collaborate well with a team.
- Self-starter with genuine curiosity.

### Bonus Points
- Hands-on Kubernetes or Docker experience from labs, personal projects, or open-source contributions.
- Exposure to cloud platforms like AWS, Azure, or GCP.
- Experience with infrastructure-as-code tools such as Terraform or Ansible.
- Solid networking fundamentals: TCP/IP, DNS, VLANs, and firewalls.
- Familiarity with observability tools like Prometheus or Grafana.

### Why Join
Enterprise-grade infrastructure; senior engineers with Kubevirt, Kubernetes, HA clustering expertise; DevOps practices, CI/CD pipelines, modern automation tools.
