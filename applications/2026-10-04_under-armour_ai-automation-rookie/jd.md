### Placement Area: AI Automation
As an AI Automation Rookie, you'll help identify opportunities to simplify processes, improve efficiency, and enhance the teammate experience through the use of artificial intelligence and automation tools. Working alongside business, technology, and data teams, you'll support the design, testing, and implementation of innovative solutions that streamline workflows, reduce manual effort, and enable smarter decision-making. Through hands-on projects and cross-functional collaboration, you'll gain exposure to emerging technologies and how AI is transforming the way work gets done at Under Armour. This opportunity is ideal for students studying Computer Science, Information Systems, Engineering, Data Science, Business Analytics, or related fields.
