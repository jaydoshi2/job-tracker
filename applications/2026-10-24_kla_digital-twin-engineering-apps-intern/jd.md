# KLA — Digital Twin Engineering Apps Intern

**Group/Division:** KLA IT group

## What You'll Do
We are seeking a highly motivated Digital Twin Engineering Apps intern to support engineering and PLM process validation through test execution, defect analysis, automation, and reporting. The role provides hands-on exposure to CAD, PLM, MBSE, simulation, 3D visualization, Digital Twin creation, and AI-assisted validation while building practical experience in enterprise systems, process analysis, stakeholder communication, and cross-functional project delivery.

### Testing & Validation
- Validate technical and business process flows for system behavior, data accuracy, business rules, integrations, and digital continuity.
- Support functional, integration, regression, and UAT test cases for PLM, CAD integration, BOM, ECO/CO, MBSE, simulation, and 3D visualization.
- Prepare reusable test data for CAD files, part structures, BOM attributes, change objects, lifecycle states, simulation inputs, MBSE artifacts, and visualization scenarios.

### Defects & Evidence
- Capture clear test evidence, observations, results, and defect details to support timely review and resolution.
- Assist with defect reproduction, fix validation, regression testing, and closure confirmation.

### PLM Domain Support
- Validate PLM concepts including MBSE, simulation data, requirements traceability, product structure, change management, and 3D visualization use cases.

### Automation, AI & Reporting
- Develop lightweight automation scripts using Python, JavaScript, SQL, TCL, or similar tools to reduce manual effort in validation, data comparison, regression testing, and technical process checks.
- Support automated reporting for test execution status, defect trends, validation exceptions, process gaps, and readiness indicators.
- Use AI-assisted methods to summarize requirements, draft validation steps, identify coverage gaps, detect defect patterns, and improve documentation.

### Analysis & Collaboration
- Participate in design walkthroughs, testing reviews, defect triage discussions, and stakeholder updates.
- Analyze test results, defect trends, validation exceptions, and process observations to identify recurring patterns, root cause indicators, improvement opportunities, and readiness risks.
- Prepare concise written summaries, dashboards, and status communications for IT, engineering, business, and project stakeholders.

## Preferred Qualifications
- Pursuing a degree in Computer Science, Information Systems, Mechanical Engineering, Systems Engineering, Data Science, or a related field.
- Exposure to Python, JavaScript, Java, SQL, TCL, or similar tools for automation, validation, and reporting.
- Basic understanding of databases, APIs, integrations, metadata, logs, and data flows.
- Basic testing knowledge, including functional, integration, regression, UAT, defect tracking, and documentation.
- Exposure to CAD, PLM, PDM, BOM, engineering change, lifecycle, MBSE, simulation, or 3D visualization concepts.
- Interest in reusable scripts, test utilities, validation reports, and data comparison tools.
- Familiarity with AI for requirements analysis, test scenarios, documentation, and process improvement.
- Ability to analyze workflows, interpret results, identify gaps, and recommend improvements.

## Minimum Qualifications
Pursuing a Bachelor's or Master's degree in Computer Science, Information Systems, Mechanical Engineering, Systems Engineering, Data Science, or a related field.
