## About the role
The GN&C team designs and implements algorithms for motion planning, state estimation, flight control, actuation and contingency management for autonomous aerial robots. Candidates should show they can write and deploy software for motion planning, state estimation or low-level control on real physical systems: MPC, trajectory optimization, SLAM, Kalman filtering, geometric control, computer vision, VLA models or related techniques. Classical and state-of-the-art approaches, especially in safety-critical applications.

## In this role you will
- Prototype new robot behaviors to solve business problems.
- Develop and implement motion planning, state estimation or control algorithms for Wing aircraft.
- Deploy and validate software in simulated and real environments.
- Analyze flight data to evaluate approaches at scale.
- Work with perception/CV, ML, simulation, aerodynamics/propulsion and embedded software engineers.
- Learn to build and maintain an autonomous system with 1M+ deliveries.
