- Work with data engineers and scientists to clean, prepare, and analyze structured and unstructured data
- Support development and automation of model training, evaluation, and deployment pipelines
- Explore and analyze pharmaceutical commercial datasets for data-driven insights
- Design informative visualizations and dashboards for internal stakeholders
- Apply statistical techniques including hypothesis testing, regression, and classification
- Implement and monitor ML models in production environments
- Document technical processes, models, and tools
- Experiment with new tools in data engineering and MLOps
- Participate in agile ceremonies (sprint planning, retrospectives)
